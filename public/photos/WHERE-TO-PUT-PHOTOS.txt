Bits & Bots — where to add photos
=================================

Project folder:
  public/photos/

Useful locations:

  logo/fll-logo.png            — header & footer logo
  site/hero-bits-and-bots.png  — home page hero
  gallery/                     — Photo Gallery page at /gallery
  events/                      — Events page banners by type
  outreach/                    — Outreach page photos

Coach and team headshots are uploaded in Admin (not these folders).

After copying photos, refresh http://localhost:8080/ (keep npm run dev running).

Photos straight from a phone or camera are large (5–15 MB) and make the site slow.
After adding new ones, shrink them all with:

  python3 scripts/optimize-photos.py
